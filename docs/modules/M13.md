# Module M13 — Backoffice Financeiro e Conciliação (`apps/admin-web`, `apps/api`, `packages/domain`)

## 1. Scope & Architecture
- **Balanced Double-Entry Journals (`buildBalancedOrderCaptureJournal`)**:
  - Every captured order produces an immutable journal: `PSP_CLEARING_RECEIVABLE` (+ `PLATFORM_PROMO_SUBSIDY_EXPENSE` when the platform funds a discount) on the debit side; `MERCHANT_PAYABLE:<orgId>`, `COURIER_PAYABLE_POOL`, `PLATFORM_COMMISSION_REVENUE`, `PLATFORM_SERVICE_FEE_REVENUE` on the credit side.
  - Throws `LEDGER_UNBALANCED_JOURNAL` if debits ≠ credits. All amounts are integer BRL centavos.
  - Database side: `public.ledger_account_balances_v` (`security_invoker = true`) aggregates debits/credits per account under RLS.
- **GMV vs Net Platform Revenue (`buildFinancialBackofficeModel`)**:
  - GMV = `subtotalCents + addonsCents`; Net Platform Revenue = `platformCommissionCents + serviceFeeCents`. Merchant and courier payables are reported as separate liabilities, never as revenue.
  - Localized notice `finance.gmv_vs_revenue.notice` (`en` / `pt-BR`).
- **PSP Reconciliation (`POST /v1/finance/reconciliation-runs`, `evaluatePspReconciliationRun`)**:
  - Matches internal captured totals vs PSP-reported captures per order; reports `matchedCount`, `discrepancyCount`, `discrepancyTotalCents` (absolute sum) down to 1 centavo.
  - Requires `finance` or `tech_admin` + MFA `aal2`; idempotency key required; run is audited (`PSP_RECONCILIATION_RUN_COMPLETED`).
- **Settlement Payout Execution (`POST /v1/finance/settlements/:settlementId/execute`, `evaluateSettlementPayoutExecution`)**:
  - Requires `finance`/`tech_admin` + MFA `aal2`.
  - **Segregation of duties**: the actor who approved the bank account cannot execute the payout (`403 SETTLEMENT_SEGREGATION_OF_DUTIES_VIOLATION`).
  - **Honest provider status**: when the PSP is `BLOCKED_MISSING_CREDENTIALS`/`DISABLED`, the settlement becomes `BLOCKED_NO_PSP` with `externalPayoutReference = null` — no bank transfer is simulated. `PAID_CONFIRMED` requires a real PSP payout reference.

## 2. Known Limitations
- Settlements, reconciliation runs and journals in `apps/api` are held in in-memory stores when no repository adapter is supplied; the Supabase tables/RLS (`settlements`, `reconciliation_runs`, `ledger_*`) exist but the persistence adapter is not yet wired.
- Real payouts remain blocked until PSP credentials are configured.
