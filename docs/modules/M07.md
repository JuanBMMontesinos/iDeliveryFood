# Module M07 — Portal Web do Parceiro: Gestão do Estabelecimento (`apps/partner-web`)

## 1. Scope & Architecture
- **Store Settings & Pause Management (`PATCH /v1/stores/{storeId}/settings`)**: Allows `partner_owner`, `partner_manager`, and `partner_operator` to pause/unpause a store (with mandatory `pauseReason`), update preparation time bounds (`prepTimeMinMinutes <= prepTimeMaxMinutes`), and update `minOrderCents` in integer BRL centavos.
- **Operating Hours, Exceptions & Service Areas**: Governed by `store_hours_manage_partner`, `store_hour_exceptions_manage_partner`, and `store_service_areas_manage_partner` RLS policies in Supabase (`dwsoochnfxldfvcaijie`).
- **Financial & Bank Account Governance (`POST /v1/stores/{storeId}/bank-account-change-request`)**:
  - Restricted strictly to `partner_owner` with MFA step-up (`aal2`).
  - Creates a `PENDING` approval record (`target_type = 'BANK_ACCOUNT'`) storing only `accountLast4` in audit metadata.
  - Never triggers immediate payout liquidation (`immediatePayoutTriggered: false`).
- **Multi-Tenant & Role Segregation**: `partner_operator` and `partner_catalog` are blocked from accessing `ledger_accounts`, `settlements`, or another tenant's `organization_id`.
