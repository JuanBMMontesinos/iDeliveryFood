# Module M16 — Administração Central, Acessos e Cadastros (`apps/admin-web`)

## 1. Scope & Architecture
- **Role-Scoped Backoffice Navigation (`buildAdminRoleNavigationModules`)**:
  - Segregates internal modules (`CADASTRE_AND_APPROVALS`, `LOGISTICS_DISPATCH_CONSOLE`, `SUPPORT_AND_DISPUTES`, `FINANCE_LEDGER_AND_SETTLEMENTS`, `COMMERCIAL_CONTRACTS`, `GROWTH_PROMOTIONS`, `RISK_COMPLIANCE_AND_LGPD`, `IAM_AND_PROVIDER_GOVERNANCE`) according to server-side `staff_scopes`.
- **Self-Grant Prohibition (`trg_guard_staff_scope_self_grant` & `POST /v1/admin/staff-scopes`)**:
  - Enforced both in PostgreSQL (`app_security.guard_staff_scope_self_grant` raising `42501 STAFF_SELF_GRANT_FORBIDDEN`) and in the application/API layer (`DENIED_SELF_GRANT_FORBIDDEN`).
  - Even an authenticated `tech_admin` with MFA `aal2` cannot grant or elevate administrative scopes for their own `user_id`.
- **Cadastre Approvals & Suspensions (`POST /v1/admin/governance-actions`)**:
  - Requires MFA `aal2`, authorized staff role (`operations`, `commercial`, `risk_compliance`, `tech_admin`), non-self target, and a mandatory justification (`>= 5` characters).
- **Immutable Read-Only Audit Trail (`GET /v1/admin/audit-logs` & `buildAdminAuditTrailExplorerModel`)**:
  - Exposes `audit_logs` in read-only mode (`isReadOnly: true`, `allowMutationOrDelete: false`) exclusively to authorized compliance/admin roles at `aal2`.
