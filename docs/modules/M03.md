**Status:** `CONCLUÍDO`

## 1. Deliverables Produced

1. **Multi-Actor Identity & Session Lifecycle:**
   - `public.profiles` and `public.user_preferences` linked to `auth.users(id)` with default locale `en` and switchable `pt-BR` persisted via `PATCH /v1/me/preferences`.
   - Protected profile attribute trigger `app_security.guard_profile_self_update()` preventing non-staff from modifying `status`, `mfa_enforced`, `email`, or `is_test_data` (`42501`).
   - Client `user_metadata` role-stripper (`sanitizeClientUserMetadata`) ensuring roles/scopes come exclusively from `organization_members` and `staff_scopes`.
2. **Multi-Tenant RBAC/ABAC & Partner Role Segregation:**
   - `authorizePartnerScope` + PostgreSQL RLS functions (`app_security.has_org_role`, `app_security.has_store_role`) enforce strict tenant and store boundaries.
   - Segregates operational roles (`partner_operator`, `partner_catalog`) from financial ledger & settlement views (`partner_finance`, `partner_owner`).
3. **Internal Staff Governance & MFA (`aal2`) Step-Up:**
   - `authorizeStaffAction` + `app_security.has_staff_role(..., p_require_mfa => true)` require verified JWT `aal = 'aal2'` before sensitive approvals, financial ledger inspection, or refunds.
   - Controlled local-only first-admin bootstrap script ([bootstrap-first-admin.mjs](file:///d:/IAProjects/iDeliveryFood/scripts/bootstrap-first-admin.mjs)) blocked in `production`.
4. **Single-Use Invitations, Device Revocation & LGPD Retention Holds:**
   - Invitations hashed with SHA-256 (`hashInvitationToken`), validated for expiration and single-use consumption (`POST /v1/invitations`, `POST /v1/invitations/accept`).
   - Device revocation (`POST /v1/me/devices/revoke`) with audit logging.
   - LGPD account deletion evaluator (`evaluateAccountDeletionRequest` & `POST /v1/me/privacy-requests`) enforcing `RETENTION_HOLD` when active orders, open financial disputes, or statutory fiscal ledger records exist.
   - Native mobile token storage vaults (`SecureSessionStore.kt` for Android Keystore/EncryptedSharedPreferences; `KeychainSessionVault.swift` for iOS Keychain) and allowlisted deep-link schemes (`ideliveryfood-customer://auth-callback`, `ideliveryfood-courier://auth-callback`).
