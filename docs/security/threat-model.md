# iDeliveryFood — STRIDE Threat Model & Marketplace Abuse Mitigations (`M23`)

**System:** `iDeliveryFood` Multi-Tenant Food & Grocery Delivery Marketplace  
**Database & Auth Reference:** Supabase (`dwsoochnfxldfvcaijie`)  
**Standard:** STRIDE + OWASP ASVS 4.0 Level 2  

---

## 1. Architecture Trust Boundaries

1. **Boundary TB-1 (Public Edge -> API / Web Portals):** Untrusted internet traffic from Customer Web/Mobile (`M05`/`M06`), Partner Web (`M07`/`M08`/`M09`/`M22`), Courier Mobile (`M11`), Admin Backoffice (`M16`–`M21`), and external PSP Webhooks (`M12`).
2. **Boundary TB-2 (Application Tier -> Supabase PostgreSQL & Storage):** Verified HS256 JWT identity claims (`sub`, `aal`), Row Level Security (`ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`), and signed short-lived upload tokens (`catalog-media`, `partner-kyc-docs`, `private-support-attachments`, `private-pod-evidence`).
3. **Boundary TB-3 (Transactional Outbox Worker -> External Providers):** Asynchronous worker (`apps/worker`) dispatching notifications and HMAC-signed webhooks with strict anti-SSRF validation and honest `BLOCKED_MISSING_CREDENTIALS` gating.

## 2. STRIDE Threat Matrix & Verified Controls

| Threat ID | STRIDE Category | Marketplace Attack Scenario | Implemented Technical Control | Code & Test Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TM-01** | **Tampering / Spoofing** | **Price, Weight & Coupon Fraud:** Client manipulates `price_cents`, `unit_price_cents`, `total_cents`, or scale weight in `/v1/quotes`, `/v1/orders`, or `/v1/partner/pick-tasks/*`; or launches concurrent requests to exhaust a promotion's `budget_limit_cents`. | 1. `validatePayloadAgainstMassAssignment` rejects client-supplied `price_cents`/`total_cents` (`400 MASS_ASSIGNMENT_FORBIDDEN`).<br>2. Server recalculates quotes from live `catalog_products` in integer BRL centavos (`calculateServerQuoteAndSplit`).<br>3. Atomic PostgreSQL row update (`WHERE budget_consumed_cents + $2 <= budget_limit_cents`) prevents concurrent coupon budget overrun (`409 PROMO_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`).<br>4. M22 scale pricing enforces `HALF_UP` cent rounding and blocks upward excess (`409 WEIGHING_EXCEEDS_PREAUTHORIZED_CEILING`). | `packages/config/src/index.ts` (`validatePayloadAgainstMassAssignment`), `packages/domain/src/index.ts` (`calculateServerQuoteAndSplit`, `calculateWeighedItemPriceCents`), `apps/api/test/fase-p-marketing.integration.test.ts`, `apps/api/test/fase-s-grocery.integration.test.ts` |
| **TM-02** | **Spoofing / Repudiation** | **PSP Webhook Forgery & Replay:** Attacker sends forged `payment.captured` payload to `/v1/webhooks/psp/:providerCode` or replays an old captured event to mark unpaid orders as paid. | 1. Constant-time HMAC-SHA256 verification (`computePspWebhookSignature` / `verifyAndProcessPspWebhook`) over `${timestampSeconds}.${rawPayload}`.<br>2. Strict timestamp tolerance window (`300s`).<br>3. Idempotent deduplication in `public.payment_webhook_events` (`UNIQUE(provider_code, external_event_id)`). | `packages/domain/src/index.ts` (`verifyAndProcessPspWebhook`), `apps/api/src/index.ts` (`/v1/webhooks/psp/:providerCode`), `apps/api/test/api.test.ts` |
| **TM-03** | **Elevation of Privilege / Tampering** | **Refund Abuse & Threshold Bypass:** Support L1 agent or compromised customer attempts to issue large refunds (`> 1500` centavos) or self-approve an escalated financial refund. | 1. `evaluateSupportActionRequest` enforces `platform_policies.support_auto_refund_limit_cents` (`1500` cents), escalating higher amounts to `PENDING_FINANCE`.<br>2. `decideEscalatedSupportActionRequest` requires `finance` role + MFA `aal2` + Segregation of Duties (`decidedByStaffId !== requestedByStaffId`).<br>3. Cumulative refunds cannot exceed captured order total (`evaluateRefundRequest`). | `packages/domain/src/index.ts` (`evaluateSupportActionRequest`, `decideEscalatedSupportActionRequest`), `apps/api/test/fase-n-support.integration.test.ts` |
| **TM-04** | **Information Disclosure / Tampering** | **Cross-Tenant Leakage (Store A vs. Store B):** Partner member of Organization 101 / Store 201 attempts to read or mutate catalog, orders, finance, or grocery picking tasks belonging to Organization 102 / Store 202. | 1. `authorizePartnerScope` enforces organization and unit-level membership (`organization_members`).<br>2. PostgreSQL RLS helper `app_security.has_store_role` and `app_security.has_org_role` enforce tenant isolation at the database layer.<br>3. Support internal notes (`is_internal_note = true`) are stripped for `CUSTOMER` and `PARTNER` actors (`filterSupportCaseViewForActor`). | `packages/authz/src/index.ts` (`authorizePartnerScope`), `supabase/migrations/20261103155919_m02_rls_grants_storage_and_realtime.sql`, `apps/api/test/api.test.ts`, `apps/api/test/fase-s-grocery.integration.test.ts` |
| **TM-05** | **Information Disclosure** | **Premature Exposure of Customer Address to Courier:** Unassigned courier browsing dispatch offers attempts to scrape exact customer street names, house numbers, or apartment complements before accepting the delivery offer. | `buildCourierDispatchOfferView` and `GET /v1/logistics/offers` strip `dropoffFullAddress` and expose only `dropoffNeighborhoodOnly` while the offer is `PENDING`; exact street address is revealed only after transactional offer acceptance (`acceptDeliveryOfferTransactional`). | `packages/domain/src/index.ts` (`acceptDeliveryOfferTransactional`), `apps/api/src/index.ts`, `apps/api/test/api.test.ts` |
| **TM-06** | **Tampering / Elevation of Privilege** | **Merchant Bank Account & Settlement Hijack:** Compromised `partner_operator` attempts to change payout bank account, or a single staff member approves a bank account change and immediately executes settlement payout to that account. | 1. Bank account change requests (`createBankAccountChangeRequest`) require `partner_owner` + MFA `aal2` and enter `PENDING` compliance status without triggering payout.<br>2. Settlement execution (`evaluateSettlementPayoutExecution`) enforces `finance` + MFA `aal2` + Segregation of Duties (`bankAccountApprovedByActorId !== actorId`). | `packages/domain/src/index.ts` (`createBankAccountChangeRequest`, `evaluateSettlementPayoutExecution`), `apps/partner-web/test/partner-web.test.ts`, `apps/api/test/api.test.ts` |
| **TM-07** | **Elevation of Privilege** | **Administrative Privilege Escalation & Self-Grant:** Staff user injects `role: "tech_admin"` via mass assignment, spoofs `user_metadata.role`, or attempts to grant `finance`/`tech_admin` scope to their own `user_id`. | 1. `sanitizeClientUserMetadata` strips all client-writable role/scope claims.<br>2. `validatePayloadAgainstMassAssignment` rejects unexpected `role`, `staff_scopes`, `is_admin`.<br>3. `evaluateStaffScopeGrant` and PostgreSQL trigger `app_security.guard_staff_scope_self_grant()` block self-grant (`targetUserId === actorId` -> `DENIED_SELF_GRANT_FORBIDDEN`) and require MFA `aal2` + mandatory justification. | `packages/authz/src/index.ts`, `packages/domain/src/index.ts` (`evaluateStaffScopeGrant`), `supabase/migrations/20261103171252_m07_m08_m16_partner_ops_and_admin_governance.sql`, `apps/admin-web/test/admin-web.test.ts` |
| **TM-08** | **Spoofing / Brute Force** | **Delivery 4-Digit OTP Enumeration:** Courier or attacker brute-forces 4-digit delivery confirmation codes on `POST /v1/logistics/tasks/:id/complete-otp` to mark undelivered orders as `DELIVERED`. | 1. Only SHA-256 OTP hashes (`hashDeliveryConfirmationCode`) are stored in `orders.delivery_confirmation_code_hash`.<br>2. `SlidingWindowRateLimiter` classifies `/v1/logistics/tasks/:id/complete-otp` in the `OTP_VERIFY` bucket (default max 5 attempts/60s window), returning `429 TOO_MANY_REQUESTS` with `Retry-After`.<br>3. Explainable risk rule `COURIER_REPEATED_OTP_FAILURES` opens a `risk_alerts` record for human review (`M21`). | `packages/config/src/index.ts` (`SlidingWindowRateLimiter`), `packages/domain/src/index.ts` (`verifyDeliveryConfirmationCode`, `evaluateExplainableRiskRule`), `apps/api/test/fase-t-security.integration.test.ts` |
| **TM-09** | **Information Disclosure / SSRF** | **Outbound Webhook SSRF & Storage Active-Content XSS:** Partner registers a webhook pointing to cloud metadata (`https://169.254.169.254/latest/meta-data/`) or private VPC (`10.0.0.0/8`, `127.0.0.1`), or uploads an `.svg`/`.html` XSS payload disguised as `image/png`. | 1. `validateExternalWebhookUrlAgainstSsrf` blocks non-HTTPS schemes, loopback, RFC 1918 private networks, link-local/IMDS (`169.254.0.0/16`), and internal hostnames.<br>2. `validateStorageUploadSecurity` blocks executable/active-content extensions (`.svg`, `.html`, `.js`, `.exe`) and enforces strict extension-to-MIME correspondence. | `packages/config/src/index.ts` (`validateExternalWebhookUrlAgainstSsrf`, `validateStorageUploadSecurity`), `apps/worker/src/index.ts`, `apps/api/test/fase-t-security.integration.test.ts` |
